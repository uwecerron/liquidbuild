Questions, answered

What is design-build?

One contract covers design, engineering, permits and construction. The team drawing the project is the team building it, so price and schedule are set early.

Is design-build cheaper?

It usually reduces surprises: the budget is checked during design instead of after bids come back, and permits and site work can start while drawings finish.

What do you design-build?

Custom homes, additions, pools, restaurant and retail interiors, school renovations and industrial buildings.
