Questions, answered

What does Liquid Build do?

Liquid Build is an AI-powered general contractor and developer in South Florida. We develop single-family communities and build custom homes, commercial build-outs, schools and data centers across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties. We use AI to answer faster, estimate faster and keep permits moving, and experienced builders run every job.

Where do you work?

Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties. That covers Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach and the towns in between, including Key Biscayne, Homestead and the Redland.

How fast do you reply to a quote request?

Within one business day. Call or text 305-833-5025 for anything urgent.

Can my AI assistant get an estimate from you?

Yes. Agents can connect to our MCP server at https://liquid-build.com/api/mcp for company info, ballpark estimates, project grounding and quote requests. See https://liquid-build.com/agents.
