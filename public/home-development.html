Questions, answered

Do you develop land you don't own yet?

Yes. We run feasibility before you close: yield, sitework budget, utilities, flood zone and a realistic schedule, so you know what the land can become.

How many homes have you developed?

Our team at Shores Development has developed, built and sold more than 10,000 single-family and condo units since 1950, including Redland Ranches, Toscana Estates and Las Palmas.

Who handles permits and plats?

We coordinate plats, zoning and building permits with our sister company Liquid Permit.
