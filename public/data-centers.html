Questions, answered

What limits a data center schedule?

Usually power: utility capacity, interconnection timelines and long-lead electrical equipment. We screen sites by available megawatts and time to energize before design.

What part of a data center do you build?

Site and power diligence, sitework and pads, shell and core, and coordination of switchgear, generator yards and cooling plant with your engineers.

Do you build smaller edge sites?

Yes, including prefabricated modules and small edge facilities.
