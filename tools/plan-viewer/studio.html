What is traced vs assumed?

The layout is manually reconstructed from the lower unit on page 11. The printed 36′6″ span supplies the scale; endpoints, wall centerlines and openings remain approximate.

Heights, wall thickness, window sills, opening heads and porch geometry are schematic. Stairs are omitted. This is not an automatic PDF-to-BIM conversion or a construction-ready model.

Room colors and labels are a fixed reference; they do not recompute room boundaries after wall edits. GLB units are meters; editable JSON uses feet. Source and assumptions accompany the model.
