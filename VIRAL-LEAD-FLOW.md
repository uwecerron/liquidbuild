Liquid Build: Viral Lead Flow
Sep 25, 2026 · @uwe
Post short videos that answer "what will it cost to build in South Florida," send every viewer to the free ballpark tool, and let the CRM text them back in seconds. Aim for your first 20 qualified leads in 14 days.
The loop
Every video ends with one ask: get your free ballpark. The tool gives value in seconds, the form captures the lead, and the CRM texts them before they close the tab.
1. Video. A 20 to 40 second clip on Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook. It answers one money question for South Florida.
2. Comment. The caption says "Comment BUILD and your city." Comments push the video to more people. An auto-DM (ManyChat on Instagram) sends the estimator link.
3. Ballpark. They get a price range at liquid-build.com in 30 seconds. No login.
4. Lead. The form fills in their ballpark automatically. They tap "Request a quote."
5. Text. They get an automatic text right away. You get a text and an email.
6. CRM. The lead lands on the board with the video it came from. Call within an hour.
7. Reply video. Pick the best comments and answer them in a new video ("@maria asked what a 2nd story costs in Hialeah"). That feeds step 1 again.
Why it spreads: price videos get saved and shared, comment prompts boost reach, and reply videos turn every commenter into the next piece of content.

Three scripts to shoot this week
Each one runs about 30 seconds. Film on a phone, vertical, with captions on. Read the price off the estimator on screen, and only after you have put your real prices in it.
1. "What it costs to build in Miami"
• Hook (0 to 3 s), standing on a lot: "Here's what it costs to build a house in Miami right now."
• Body (3 to 22 s), screen recording of the estimator: "3,000 square feet, mid-range finishes, Coral Gables. [Range from the tool]. About 40 to 70 weeks to build. Land and permits are extra."
• Turn (22 to 27 s): "Most people are off by six figures before they talk to anyone."
• Ask (27 to 30 s): "Comment BUILD and your city, and I'll send you the free tool."
2. "Your windows might be illegal"
• Hook, pointing at a window: "If you live in Broward or Miami-Dade, these windows might not pass inspection."
• Body: "Both counties are in the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. Windows, doors and roofing need products approved for it. Swap in the wrong ones and your permit fails."
• Ask: "Planning a remodel? Comment WINDOWS and I'll send the checklist."
3. "From empty land to 57 homes"
• Hook, drone shot of bare land: "This was a field."
• Body, cut to the aerial of homes going up, then finished streets: "We took it from raw land to 57 homes in the Redland. Roads, drainage, water, sewer, then every house."
• Ask: "Got land? Comment LAND and I'll tell you what it could hold."
Use the same hook structure every time. Name a place, name a number, promise the answer in the first three seconds.

Prompts to make ads
Copy these into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Runway or ElevenLabs. Swap the parts in [brackets].
Two rules first. Anything that says "we built this" must use your real photos and footage. Use AI images and video only for ideas, backgrounds and b-roll, and label them. Add "Made with AI" where the platform asks.
1. Ten video ad scripts (ChatGPT or Claude)
You write short vertical video ads for Liquid Build, an AI-powered general contractor and developer in South Florida (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach). The team behind it has built communities, custom homes, restaurants and campus projects since 1950. The offer: a free ballpark price in 30 seconds at liquid-build.com, then a real quote within one business day. Audience: [homeowners in Broward who want to add a second story]. Write 10 scripts, 20 to 30 seconds each. For each give: a hook under 10 words that names a place or a number, what's on screen second by second, the voiceover, on-screen captions, and the ending "Comment BUILD and your city." Plain words, short sentences, no em dashes, no hype words like "revolutionary" or "game-changing". Don't invent prices; write [PRICE FROM TOOL] where a number goes.
2. Spanish versions
Rewrite these scripts in natural Miami Spanish, the way a Cuban or Venezuelan homeowner in Hialeah or Doral would talk. Keep construction terms people actually use (like "permiso", "ventanas de impacto", "segundo piso"). Keep each under 30 seconds read aloud. End with "Comenta CONSTRUIR y tu ciudad."
3. Static image ad (ChatGPT image, Gemini or Canva AI)
Photorealistic aerial photo at golden hour of a new single-family neighborhood under construction in South Florida. Flat land, royal palms, concrete block homes with barrel tile roofs, some finished and some framed, fresh asphalt streets. Clear sky with empty space across the top third for a headline. No text, no logos, no people's faces. Vertical 4:5.
Then add in Canva: headline "What will it cost to build?", subline "Free ballpark in 30 seconds", button "liquid-build.com". Label it as an illustration.
4. B-roll video clip (Gemini Veo or Runway)
8-second vertical 9:16 video. A drone slowly rises over an empty lot of flat Florida land with palm trees at sunrise, then tilts forward to reveal a row of new concrete block homes with tile roofs. Realistic, natural light, no text, no people.
Other shots to try: "close-up of a hurricane impact window being installed in a concrete block wall", "a pool being poured in a Florida backyard, overhead", "a restaurant dining room under construction, lights being hung".
5. Voiceover (ElevenLabs)
Pick your cloned voice. Paste the script. Settings to start: stability in the middle, style low, speed normal. Direction to add before the text: "Calm, friendly, confident. A contractor talking to a neighbor, not an announcer." Generate three takes and keep the one that sounds most like you.
6. Google Search ads
Write a Google Responsive Search Ad for Liquid Build, a general contractor in [Miami]. Give 15 headlines of 30 characters or less and 4 descriptions of 90 characters or less. Include the city, "Free ballpark estimate", "Licensed general contractor", "New homes, additions, build-outs" and "Reply in 1 business day". No em dashes, no claims we can't back up, no prices.
Searches to bid on first: "general contractor [city]", "build a house on my lot [county]", "home addition contractor [city]", "restaurant build out contractor [city]".
7. Meta ad text
Write 5 versions of Facebook and Instagram ad text for the video above. Primary text under 125 characters, a headline under 40 characters, and the button "Get quote". Plain words, one question up front, no em dashes, no emojis in the headline.
If Ads Manager puts the ad in a special category like Housing, accept it. That limits some targeting, so target by county instead.
8. SideShift creator brief
Liquid Build is an AI-powered general contractor in South Florida. We want a 20 to 30 second vertical video, in your own voice, filmed at your home or a street in [Miami-Dade]. Show yourself trying the free ballpark tool at liquid-build.com on your phone (screen record it), react to the price, and say one real reason you'd use it. End with "Comment BUILD and your city." Post from your account with #ad or the paid-partnership tag, and use this link: [tracked link]. Send the draft to us for approval before posting. Don't promise prices, dates or discounts.
Content flywheel
The goal: one hour of filming a week turns into a month of posts, and every lead and comment tells you what to film next.
1. Capture (1 hour a week). One site visit. Film a drone pass, a walk-through, two or three short talking clips, and the crew at work. Save everything to one folder named by date and job.
2. Produce. Edit in CapCut. Put your ElevenLabs voice on anything you didn't say on camera. Add captions.
3. Multiply. Each shoot becomes: 5 short videos (one per pillar), Spanish versions of each through ElevenLabs dubbing, one Google Business Profile post with photos, and one project entry on the website. Photos on the site and on Google Maps help search as much as the videos help social.
4. Distribute. Post one a day across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook. Send the best clips to your SideShift creators so they remix them in their own voice with their tracked link.
5. Capture leads. Comment keyword, ManyChat DM, estimator, quote form, automatic text, CRM. This part already runs on its own.
6. Learn. Every Friday, open the CRM and sort leads by source. Which hook, which city, which creator brought quote requests?
7. Amplify. Put ad money only behind the video with the most saves and the most leads per 1,000 views.
8. Feed back. The questions people ask in comments, DMs and calls become next week's scripts. The best ones also go on the site as FAQs, which is what Google and AI assistants quote when someone asks about building in South Florida.
